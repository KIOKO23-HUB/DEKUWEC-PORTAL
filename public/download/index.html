How to install

From tap to signed in, in about a minute

Android will interrupt you once or twice on the way. That's normal for an app installed outside the Play Store — here's exactly what you'll see.

  1. 1

    Tap the download button

    Your phone saves DEKUWEC.apk (4.7 MB). Chrome may warn that “this type of file can harm your device” — that's the generic wording for any app file. Choose Download anyway.

    Download now
  2. 2

    Open the downloaded file

    Pull down your notifications and tap the file, or open Files → Downloads and tap DEKUWEC.apk. Don't worry if you can't find the folder — the notification is the quickest route.

  3. 3

    Allow installs from this source

    Android blocks unknown apps by default. You'll see “For your security, your phone is not allowed to install unknown apps from this source.” Tap Settings → turn on Allow from this source → press Back.

    This only allows the app you're installing right now. You can switch it back off afterwards.

  4. 4

    Install, then open

    Tap Install. If Play Protect says “Unsafe app blocked”, tap More details → Install anyway. Then open DEKUWEC and sign in with the same account you use on the website.

    Signing in once is enough — the app keeps you logged in after that.

Obstructions to expect

Warnings you'll see — and what to do

None of these mean something is wrong. They're Android's standard caution for any app not installed from the Play Store.

“Your phone is not allowed to install unknown apps”

Why: Android blocks apps that don't come from the Play Store until you opt in for that one source.

Fix: In the dialog tap Settings, enable Allow from this source for Chrome (or Files), then press Back and continue.

“Unsafe app blocked” from Play Protect

Why: Play Protect flags anything Google hasn't scanned in the Play Store. Our app isn't published there, so it appears in that list.

Fix: Tap More details → Install anyway. The app is built and signed by the club, and only requests internet access.

“App not installed”

Why: Usually an older copy installed from a different file, or not enough free storage.

Fix: Free up a little space, delete any previous DEKUWEC install, then install again. Updates over an existing install work normally — no uninstall needed.

“You're offline” inside the app

Why: The portal is live content, so it needs a connection the first time it loads.

Fix: Nothing — check your data or Wi-Fi and the app returns to the portal by itself, or tap Try again on that screen.

Asked to sign in again

Why: For everyone's safety the login occasionally expires.

Fix: Sign in once more — use the same email you use on the website. You stay signed in until the session expires or you uninstall.

“Can't open file” or a partial download

Why: The download was interrupted, so the file is incomplete.

Fix: Delete the file and download again over Wi-Fi. Confirm it's about 4.7 MB before opening it.

Questions

Things members ask

Is it free?

Yes. The app is a member service of the club — there's nothing to buy, no ads and no subscription.

Do I need a Google Play account?

No. The app installs directly from this page, so you don't need Play Store access or a Google account.

Does it replace the website?

No — it's the same portal, opened in a phone-shaped window. Your account, membership and payments work identically in both.

Can I use it on iPhone?

Not yet — this build is Android only. iPhone members should keep using the website.

How do I update to a newer version?

Come back to this page and download again. Installing over your existing app keeps you signed in — no need to uninstall first.

Will it use a lot of data?

No. It streams the same pages you'd see in a browser and caches images, so it uses roughly what the website does.

Why isn't it on the Play Store?

Play Store review takes time and costs a developer account. Publishing directly means members get new versions the day they're ready — the trade-off is the install warning you'll see.
